ELP PRACTICE TEST (unofficial, in the style of WIDA ACCESS)
==========================================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
- ELP-Practice-Test.html  -> the test. Double-click it to open it in Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge).
- images/                 -> put the pictures here. The test works without them (it shows simple emoji pictures).
- ELP-image-prompts.csv   -> one row per picture: the exact file name to use and an AI-image prompt.
                             Priority 1 = big scene pictures (74). Priority 2 = small answer-choice pictures (65).

SHARING WITH OTHER TEACHERS
Share the WHOLE folder (zip it, or share it from Google Drive or OneDrive). Each teacher downloads it,
unzips it, and opens ELP-Practice-Test.html in Chrome. Nothing needs to be installed and no account is needed.
(If the HTML file is moved away from the images folder, the test still works but shows emoji pictures.)

BEFORE TESTING (each computer)
1. On the start page, open Teacher settings and click "Test the voices". Adjust the voice speed if needed.
   "Choose voices" lets you pick the narrator, teacher, and student voices. Chrome's
   "Google US English" and Edge's "Natural" voices sound best.
2. Click "Test the microphone" and choose Allow.
3. Students should use headphones. For Speaking, a headset with a microphone works best.

DURING THE TEST
- Order: Listening (12 questions), Reading (12), Speaking (4 tasks), Writing (2 tasks). About 30–35 minutes.
- Listening and Reading stop early if a student misses all 3 questions in a part. This saves time for
  beginning students.
- Speaking records the student's voice so the teacher can listen later. Chrome also tries to turn the
  speech into text. Note: Chrome's speech-to-text sends audio to Google to process it.

AFTER THE TEST
- The score report shows Listening, Reading, Speaking, and Writing levels (1.0–6.0) plus Oral Language,
  Literacy, Comprehension, and Overall, with approximate scale scores.
- For the most accurate Speaking and Writing scores, the teacher should use the rubric menus on the score report
  (Linguistic complexity, Vocabulary usage, Language control). Until then, the built-in estimate is used.
- Click "Download results (.csv)" to keep a copy. Results also stay in that browser on that computer
  (start page -> Saved results -> Download all).

ADDING PICTURES
- Start page -> Teacher settings -> "Picture checklist & prompts" shows every picture, where it is used,
  its prompt, and whether it has been added yet.
- Save each picture as .png with the exact file name, inside the images folder.
  Scenes: 4:3 landscape. Answer choices and portraits: square.

IMPORTANT
This is unofficial practice material written to the WIDA ELD Standards Framework (2020). It is not made or
endorsed by WIDA. Scores are practice estimates and should not be used for placement or exit decisions.
